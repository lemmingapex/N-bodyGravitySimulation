How to play

Every body attracts every other body by Newton's law of gravitation. Launch planets, drop in whole scenarios, and watch orbits, collisions and slingshots play out.

Mouse & trackpad

  • Click: add a planet (in a circular orbit when Tap = circular orbit is on)
  • Click, pull back & release: launch a planet like a slingshot
  • Scenario tool (pick one in the toolbar), then click: drop in a copy of that scenario; pull back to launch it
  • Click a body: inspect it (mass, speed, orbit) and follow or delete it
  • Wheel / pinch: zoom around the cursor
  • Right-drag, middle-drag or Shift-drag: pan

Touch

  • Tap: add a planet · pull back & release: launch it
  • Tap a body: inspect, follow or delete it
  • Two fingers: pan and pinch-zoom
  • Use the toolbar to switch between planets, scenarios and pan

Under the hood

Choose between Euler, leapfrog (Verlet), Yoshida 4th-order and Runge–Kutta 4 integrators, and exact O(n²) gravity or the Barnes–Hut O(n log n) approximation. Adaptive substeps automatically shrink the time step during close encounters. The ΔE readout shows how well energy is conserved. Physics runs in background threads, using every spare CPU core for big systems, and bodies are drawn with WebGL, so tens of thousands of bodies stay smooth.
